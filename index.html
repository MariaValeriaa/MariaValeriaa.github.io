03 / projects

Selected research projects

Aug 2025 – 2026

KNN-guided candidate restriction for large-scale TSP

Binghamton University · Advisor: Dr. Sreenath Chalil Madathil

Evaluated k-nearest-neighbor candidate restriction across exact and heuristic solvers (DFJ, Concorde, LKH, SOM) on 118 TSPLIB instances with nonparametric statistical tests. The restriction cut median DFJ runtime by about 50% and raised the LKH optimality rate from 87.2% to 93.2%.

PythonGurobiDFJConcordeLKHstatistical tests
2026

Continuous improvement in electronics manufacturing (Celestica)

Binghamton University at Celestica Inc. · Advisor: Dr. Yingge Zhou

Cleanroom waste-separation redesign (Best of Site award), cable kitting redesigned from per-rack warehouse carts into a pull system, cycle-time studies, 5S audits, hour-by-hour boards and a SimPy simulation. This work is the basis of my M.S. thesis.
